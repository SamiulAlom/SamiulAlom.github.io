About

I finished a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering at IUBAT in January 2026 (CGPA 3.74/4.00). My thesis, an explainable three-layer ensemble for early suicide-risk prediction, was published in Engineering Reports. Since February 2026 I have been an Adjunct Research Associate at Miyan Research Institute, working with more than eight faculty members on experiments, model evaluation and manuscripts. In October 2026 I joined Brilliancy Research in Dhaka as a software engineer and research assistant.

In my clinical, imaging and language work I pair each prediction with a feature-attribution view (SHAP or LIME) so the user can see which inputs drove it. I use ensembles, CNNs, vision transformers and federated learning, and I build the software around the models in React, TypeScript, Flask and Django.
